    Agentic workflow

    How I lead spec-driven development: one reviewed spec, carried through every stage by agent skills, with people approving each step.

    1. SpecReviewed by people. Requirements and low-level design in one source of truth.
    2. BuildSkills turn the spec into code; scripts keep schemas and payloads deterministic.
    3. TestAcceptance criteria become Gherkin and black-box checks.
    4. ObserveAlerts and metrics generated as code.
    5. VerifyChecklists and evals gate every skill before the central registry.
    • Started with sequence diagrams to code, then grew it stage by stage to data and NFRs, tuned on feedback from greenfield and brownfield teams.
    • Right-sized models per task, and deterministic logic wherever a model isn’t needed.
    • Spread it through AI accelerator workshops and with principal engineers. Teams now start from skills instead of from scratch.
    • 70% faster development, across 30+ functionalities and 70+ APIs and events.
